Working with the task board

  Press n to jump to the composer, then Enter to add what you typed.
  Press r to have the runtime pick a task for you at random.
  Press t to start or pause the session clock.
  Press 1, 2 or 3 to switch between All, Active and Done.
  Press Esc to clear the notice and the current pick.

This text was fetched with Cmd::HttpGet. The response arrives back in the
update loop as Result<String, String>, so the panel you are reading it in
had to handle the failure case before it could render this one.
